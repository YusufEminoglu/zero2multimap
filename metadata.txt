about=02Multimap splits the QGIS map into 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 panels that pan and zoom together, so scenarios, years or indicators can be read side by side instead of toggled on and off.
    Click once to inspect vector attributes and raster values at the same point in every panel. A difference view marks what was added in green and removed in red. Each panel shows its feature count and polygon area, measured on the ellipsoid so the figure is true ground area in any CRS. Ready templates (Before and After, 4-Scenario Matrix, Temporal Timeline), saved workspaces, a shared legend drawer and synchronized measuring keep comparisons consistent.
    Export the panels to a print layout or to a self-contained HTML dashboard with legends, scale bars, three themes and a slide mode.
    Manual: https://geophilo.com/zero2multimap/manual.html
